# MedChef ✳️🩺

> Good food. Safer choices. Screen everyday meals and grocery lists against your prescriptions to spark better conversations with your care team.

Built for **DubHacks 2026**.

---

## 💡 The Problem

Many seniors and individuals managing chronic health conditions are unaware that ordinary, everyday foods can interact dangerously with prescription medications. 
- **Grapefruit** inhibits enzymes required to break down statins (like simvastatin), leading to toxic blood concentration levels.
- **Leafy greens** rich in Vitamin K (like spinach or kale) counteract anticoagulants like warfarin.
- **High-potassium items** (like bananas or salt substitutes) can cause hyperkalemia when combined with ACE inhibitors like lisinopril.

Navigating these dietary minefields is stressful, and critical warnings are often buried in dense pharmacy paperwork. **MedChef** brings clarity to mealtime by checking pantry items, recipes, and groceries against your medications before you cook.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **Food–Drug Interaction Screening:** Cross-references common medications (e.g., warfarin, statins, ACE inhibitors) with foods to flag potential contraindications.
- **On-Device OCR Photo Input:** Snap or upload a photo of a pill bottle, grocery receipt, or restaurant menu—processed client-side in the browser for privacy.
- **Safer Recipe & Meal Suggestions:** Recommends alternative ingredients and balanced meals tailored around your medication regimen.
- **Care Team Check-In Export:** Generates clear talking points and flagged items to bring directly to your doctor or pharmacist.
